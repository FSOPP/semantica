# Stakeholder Analysis — Semantica

> People and process are the part of architecture a working tree knows least about. Almost everything here is a question.

## Stakeholder map

What the repository shows: 78 contributors across 2,988 commits, with one contributor owning 54.6% of files and the next two 9.2% and 5.6%. 698 of 976 git-attributed files have a bus factor of 1.

I: contribution share is not a stakeholder map — basis: git attribution measures who last touched a file, not who decides, funds or depends on the project.

Community process files exist: `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `GOVERNANCE`-adjacent material in `GROWTH.md`, `SUPPORT.md`, and GitHub discussion and issue templates including a `grant.md` [D: .github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/grant.md:1].

OPEN: who are the users? No customer, adopter or deployment is named anywhere in the tree.
OPEN: what does the grant issue template imply about funding, and who administers it?
